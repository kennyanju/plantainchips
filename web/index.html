03 / Architectural Comparison
Pure Rust Local Execution vs Traditional SaaS CNAPP
Evaluation Dimension Traditional SaaS CNAPPs CloudSecure (Pure Rust Suite)
Data Privacy & Egress Requires shipping all cloud config, IAM state, and DB logs to vendor SaaS. Zero Egress. 100% local assessment on your workstation or air-gapped CI runner.
Runtime Footprint Heavy JVM/Node wrappers or 250MB Electron desktop shells consuming 2GB RAM. Lightweight Native Rust. < 15 MB binaries, < 80 MB idle RAM, 120 FPS wgpu rendering.
Agent & Automation DX Opaque web portals and rate-limited REST endpoints. 4-Way Deterministic Bus. Full parity across GUI, CLI, JSON-RPC, and Model Context Protocol.
Query Language Proprietary SaaS DSL locked behind annual enterprise subscriptions. Open RQL. Zero-copy winnow parser and planner running locally on open schemas.
Licensing & Freedom Monolithic multi-tenant SaaS contracts starting at five figures. 100% Free & Open Source under Apache 2.0 and MIT licenses.
